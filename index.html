Clinical Terminology Reference

Medical Terms Glossary

Plain-language clinical definitions for aerospace medicine abbreviations and physiological measures tracked in the journal.

SANS Ocular & Neurological
Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome

A constellation of ocular structural changes observed in long-duration astronauts, including optic disc swelling, choroidal folds, cotton-wool spots, globe flattening, and hyperopic refractive shift. Caused by persistent cephalad (headward) fluid shifts in microgravity.

LogMAR Visual Telemetry
Logarithm of the Minimum Angle of Resolution

Standard clinical scale used in aerospace research to measure visual acuity with high statistical reliability. Lower scores denote sharper vision. A score of 0.00 LogMAR corresponds to standard 20/20 Snellen vision; positive scores indicate reduced visual resolution.

ARED Countermeasures
Advanced Resistive Exercise Device

Primary resistance exercise apparatus aboard the International Space Station. Utilizes vacuum cylinders to simulate up to 600 pounds (2,670 N) of constant biomechanical load for squats, deadlifts, and heel raises to counter spaceflight bone and muscle loss.

MET Mission Time & Exertion
Mission Elapsed Time / Metabolic Equivalent

In spaceflight operations, Mission Elapsed Time is measured from launch ignition (e.g. MET Day 142). In physiological tracking, Metabolic Equivalent of Task measures energy expenditure relative to resting oxygen consumption (1 MET ≈ 3.5 mL O₂/kg/min).

ONSD Diagnostic Ultrasound
Optic Nerve Sheath Diameter

Non-invasive ultrasound measurement taken 3 mm behind the globe. Because the optic nerve sheath is continuous with intracranial cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), ONSD dilation serves as a key surrogate indicator of elevated intracranial pressure during spaceflight.
