## 2. Confidence Level Definitions

- **Established:** Published in peer-reviewed aerospace medicine literature, NASA-STD-3001 standards, or NASA Human Research Program evidence books across 20+ years of ISS missions.
- **Limited:** Documented operational or research countermeasure with clinical trials ongoing (e.g. Lower Body Negative Pressure for SANS reversal).
- **Illustrative:** Mock patient data points and simulated interactive values generated for software testing and interface demonstration.
- **Needs Verification:** Emerging findings requiring flight surgeon adjudication before operational field adoption.

---

## 3. Human Factors & Operational Safety Disclaimers

1. **Non-Pharmacologic Mandate:** All guidance tiers (Routine, Self-care, Flight surgeon, Emergency) strictly specify physical, environmental, and non-pharmacologic countermeasures (e.g. LBNP, work angle adjustments, sleep restraint elevation, CO2 diffuser checks). No medication or dosing recommendations are generated by the software.
2. **Clinical Verification Required:** Spaceflight physiological responses exhibit high inter-individual variation. Ground-based medical operations (MCC-Houston Surgeon-1 or EAC-Cologne BME) remain the authoritative clinical diagnostic authority.
